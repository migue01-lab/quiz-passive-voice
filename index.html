Part 1: Passive Voice → Active Voice
"The novel was written by George Orwell."
Correct Answer: George Orwell wrote the novel.
Explanation: The agent ("George Orwell") becomes the active subject. The past simple passive ("was written") changes to the past simple active ("wrote").
"The house will be remodeled by the architects."
Correct Answer: The architects will remodel the house.
Explanation: "The architects" perform the action in the future simple tense ("will remodel"), taking "the house" as the direct object.
"Dinner is cooked by my mother every evening."
Correct Answer: My mother cooks dinner every evening.
Explanation: The present passive ("is cooked") transforms into the simple present third-person singular ("cooks") with "my mother" as the subject.
"The reports have been reviewed by the manager."
Correct Answer: The manager has reviewed the reports.
Explanation: The present perfect passive ("have been reviewed") becomes present perfect active ("has reviewed"), agreeing with singular "the manager".
"A new song is being recorded by the band."
Correct Answer: The band is recording a new song.
Explanation: Present continuous passive ("is being recorded") changes into present continuous active ("is recording").
"The window was broken by the children."
Correct Answer: The children broke the window.
Explanation: The agent "the children" becomes the subject, and "was broken" shifts to the irregular past simple active form "broke".
Part 2: Active Voice → Passive Voice
"The gardener pruned the bushes in the park."
Correct Answer: The bushes in the park were pruned by the gardener.
Explanation: The plural direct object ("the bushes in the park") becomes the subject. We use the past plural of *to be* ("were") + past participle ("pruned") + "by the gardener".
"The company will launch a new product next month."
Correct Answer: A new product will be launched by the company next month.
Explanation: The future active form ("will launch") converts to the modal passive form ("will be launched").
"Millions of tourists visit the museum every year."
Correct Answer: The museum is visited by millions of tourists every year.
Explanation: "The museum" is singular and the tense is present simple ("visit"), so we use "is visited" followed by the agent.
"The firefighters extinguished the fire quickly."
Correct Answer: The fire was extinguished quickly by the firefighters.
Explanation: Past simple passive: singular subject "the fire" requires "was extinguished", ending with the agent "by the firefighters".
"The scientist discovered a new plant species."
Correct Answer: A new plant species was discovered by the scientist.
Explanation: "A new plant species" receives the action in past simple: "was discovered", followed by the agent.
"The committee has approved the annual budget."
Correct Answer: The annual budget has been approved by the committee.
Explanation: Present perfect active ("has approved") converts to present perfect passive ("has been approved").
